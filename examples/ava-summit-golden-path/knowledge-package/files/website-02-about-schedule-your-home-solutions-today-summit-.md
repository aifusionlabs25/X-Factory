# Built To Dominate

At Summit Home Services, we provide dependable home repairs, maintenance, remodeling, and improvement services for homeowners across Central Mississippi. From small repairs to larger home upgrades, our goal is simple: make home projects easier by providing reliable, quality service all in one place.

We specialize in the jobs homeowners often struggle to find dependable help for — the repairs, installations, and maintenance items that pile up over time. Instead of calling multiple contractors, homeowners can rely on one trusted team to handle a wide range of home service needs.

Our services include fixture and appliance installation, door and window replacement, cabinet repairs and upgrades, flooring, broken tile repair, fascia and soffit repair, TV mounting, hardware installation, punch-list items, general maintenance, and kitchen and bathroom improvements — along with many other residential repair and remodeling services.

We also partner with realtors and homeowners to complete inspection repairs and project items that may require licensed professionals, helping homes move smoothly through the buying and selling process.

With over 10 years of hands-on experience in home building, remodeling, and construction, we understand what quality workmanship looks like. Summit Home Services was built to fill the need for dependable, honest service focused on everyday repairs, maintenance, and home improvements — the jobs that matter to homeowners but often get overlooked.
